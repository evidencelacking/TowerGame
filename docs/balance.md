## Findings (unverified: causes are not checked)

- Pace is far behind our target. Mixed reaches 2 stars in year 8 and never 3; only offices reach 3 stars (year 6).
  The greedy builder explains part of it (it holds back a cash reserve and builds one room at a time), but even
  offices-only takes 4 years to reach 300 people.
- Condos-first goes bankrupt in year 2 with 16 condos built. Worth a look at condo upkeep against sale income,
  and whether the builder's early spending (a $660,000 lobby wing, a shaft, cars) is what starves it.
- Hotels stay near 325 people from year 5 on with funds unspent. Either the builder finds no reachable spot
  (suites need parking) or hotel demand stops; not diagnosed.
- Offices stop growing at about 1,840 people from year 7 with tens of millions unspent: the builder has nothing left
  it can place under its rules, not a limit of the sim. Stars 4 and 5 were never in reach of any strategy.

## Reading this

- Each row is the state at the end of that game year (12 game days, 30,600 ticks). Funds are dollars; population
  is the permanent population the star rules read.
- The builder is greedy and simple: it never repairs fires, never sets rents, never builds sky lobbies and stops
  at one local shaft's reach (floor 29) with three lobby wings. A real player does better, so these numbers are
  floors, not ceilings. Compare strategies against each other more than against the pace target.
- Our own pace target (unverified, ours): 2 stars about year 1, 3 stars by year 2 to 3, 5 stars by year 10 for
  mixed. It is measured here, not enforced.
- Status: ok, stalled (two game years of no building and no growth) or bankrupt (funds below zero; the run stops).
- Same seed, same report: the sim is deterministic and events are on, as in a normal game.
